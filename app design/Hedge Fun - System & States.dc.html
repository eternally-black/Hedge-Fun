03 · Motion language
↻
Follow + rotate
Card tracks the finger 1:1; rotation = drag·0.05°, capped ~18°.
▣
Directional overlay
A color wash grows with drag distance — lime right, red left, blue up.
◆
Stamp snap
YES/NO/SKIP label pops in at 35% with a slight overshoot.
⤴
Fling vs spring
Past threshold → flings off with momentum. Below → springs back (bounce).
▤
Next-card reveal
The card behind scales 0.93→1 as the top one leaves. Deck never empty.
✸
Bet-placed burst
Ring pulse + side stamp + floating points. Implied haptic on commit.
